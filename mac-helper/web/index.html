    Tips & troubleshooting
    
    
      
  • Sound when mirroring: when sharing a Chrome tab, keep “Also share tab audio” on. For the whole screen, turn on “Also share system audio” (macOS 14.2+ and a recent Chrome).
    • 
      
  • Best for movies: use Play a video file – it streams the file itself rather than a picture of your screen.
    • 
      
  • Choppy? Pick 720p, put the Mac and TV on the same 5 GHz Wi‑Fi, or move closer to the router.
    • 
      
  • The TV can't open the address: make sure the Mac and the Fire TV are on the same Wi‑Fi (not a guest network), and that this helper is still running. If macOS asked whether Fire TV Cast may “accept incoming network connections”, it must be Allow (System Settings → Network → Firewall → Options).
    • 
      
  • Mac asks whether Chrome may “find devices on your local network”: click Allow (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Google Chrome).
    • 
      
  • No sound on the TV: press OK on the remote – the TV browser sometimes needs a button press before it will play sound.
    • 
      
  • Press Back on the Fire TV remote to stop. Menu (☰) shows stream stats on the TV.
